# Walmart Sales & Demand Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes historical Walmart sales data using Python and Power BI to identify sales trends, compare store performance, and support supply chain demand planning.

## Tools Used
- Python (Pandas): Data cleaning and transformation
- Power BI:Data visualization and dashboard creation
- GitHub: Project documentation

## Dataset
Source: [M5 Forecasting Dataset — Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/m5-forecasting-accuracy/data)

The analysis covers 10 Walmart stores and 3 product categories using 60 days of historical sales data.

## Data Cleaning
1. Imported three CSV files using Pandas.
2. Transformed sales data from wide to long format.
3. Merged sales data with calendar and selling prices.
4. Calculated estimated revenue.
5. Exported the cleaned dataset for Power BI.

## Dashboard
The dashboard includes:
- Total Revenue
- Total Units Sold
- Daily Revenue Trend
- Revenue by Store
- Revenue by Product Category
- Interactive Filters

## Key Insights
1. Store Performance: Walmart Store 3 in California, USA generated the highest estimated revenue (approximately $1.25M).
2. Product Categories: FOODS contributed the highest revenue (approximately $4.5M).
3. Sales Trends: Daily revenue showed recurring fluctuations during the analyzed period.

## Business Recommendations
1. Improve Inventory Planning for High-Demand Products. Develop inventory replenishment plans for high-demand products, particularly in the FOODS category, to maintain product availability and reduce the risk of stockouts.
2. Optimize Inventory Management for HOBBIES. Review inventory planning for the HOBBIES category, which generated the lowest revenue. Evaluate sales volume and demand trends to identify slow-moving products and reduce the risk of overstocking.
3. Investigate Low Revenue at CA_4. Investigate why CA_4 recorded the lowest revenue among the 10 stores. Analyze product demand, customer purchasing patterns, pricing, and store-level performance to identify potential causes and opportunities for improvement.
